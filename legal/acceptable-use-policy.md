# Pip Acceptable Use Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026

This policy is part of the [Pip Terms of Service](/terms). It says what a Pip teammate may not be used for. We may pause a teammate that breaks it.

## A teammate must never

1. **Pretend to be a person.** It says it is an AI at the start of every call, text conversation and chat, and again whenever someone asks.
2. **Contact people who did not ask.** No cold calls, no cold texts, no bought or scraped lists. No contact after an opt-out.
3. **Hide a recording.** If a call is recorded, it says so at the start.
4. **Deceive or pressure.** No false urgency, fake reviews, false claims about prices or competitors, or anything unlawful.
5. **Decide things that change someone's life.** No decisions about hiring, firing, credit, lending, housing, insurance, schooling or access to health care.
6. **Give professional advice.** No medical, legal, tax or investment advice or diagnosis. It can explain your services and book a professional.
7. **Collect what it should not.** No card numbers, bank details, passwords, government ID numbers or health details in a conversation.
8. **Handle emergencies.** It tells the person to hang up and dial 911.
9. **Collect debts** or make threats about payment.
10. **Send political or election messages.**
11. **Target children.** Pip is not for services aimed at children under 13.
12. **Identify people on camera.** Counting only. No faces, no identities, no stored video, no sound.
13. **Buy outside its policy.** No orders beyond the buying policy you set, and never alcohol, tobacco, vaping products, cannabis, firearms, prescription drugs or anything that needs a license check at purchase.
14. **Get around a website's protections.** It never solves or bypasses a CAPTCHA or other block, and never uses a login you are not allowed to share.

## Businesses Pip does not serve without a separate signed agreement

- Health care providers and anyone handling protected health information.

## You must not

- Try to make a teammate break its limits, or use it to attack or overload any system.
- Give us contact lists you have no right to use.
- Resell Pip without our written agreement.
