## The short version

- Pip runs AI teammates for local businesses. They answer website chat and texts today, and phone calls once that launches.
- If you are a customer of one of those businesses, the business decides what its teammate does. Pip handles your information for that business and for nothing else.
- Every teammate tells you it is an AI in its first message.
- We do not sell personal information. We do not use it for advertising. We do not use it to train AI models.
- You can ask us or the business to see, correct or delete your information. Reply STOP to any text to stop texts.

## 4. Google user data

When a business connects Google, Pip asks only for what its teammates need:

| Permission | What a teammate does with it | What it cannot do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Send email (`gmail.send`) | Sends an email from the business's address when its job calls for it. Every email says it was sent by an AI assistant. | Read, search or delete any email. |
| Calendar events (`calendar.events`) | Checks when the business is busy and adds events. | Share calendars or change calendar settings. |
| Files Pip created (`drive.file`) | Creates one spreadsheet for the business and adds rows to it. | Open any other file in the business's Drive. |

How we treat that data:

- We use it only to do the job the business set up for its teammate.
- When a teammate checks a calendar, Pip's server receives the events from Google, strips them down to busy and free times, and passes only those times to the AI model. Event titles, guests and notes are not shown to the AI model or to the customer, and are not stored.
- We do not store email content from Google. The teammate writes the email, and it is sent.
- We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to work out creditworthiness or for lending.
- We do not use Google user data to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine learning models, and our AI provider is barred by contract from doing so.
- No person at Pip reads Google user data unless the business asks us to (for example, to fix a problem), it is needed for security or to comply with the law, or the data has been combined and stripped of identity for internal operations.
- We pass Google user data to others only as needed to provide the service the business set up (our AI provider and our connection provider), for security, to comply with the law, or as part of a merger or sale with the business's consent.

Pip's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

A business can disconnect Google at any time in the Pip Portal or in its Google account's permissions page.

## 5. Text messages

- Teammates text only people who texted the business first or who agreed to be contacted. Marketing texts go only to people with a stored consent for marketing.
- The first reply in a text conversation says the sender is an AI assistant for the business and ends with "Reply STOP to opt out."
- Reply STOP at any time to stop all texts and calls from that business's Pip number. Reply HELP for help. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies.
- **No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.** Text message opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third party, except the carriers and service providers that deliver the messages.

## 6. How AI is used

Teammates are powered by AI models from Anthropic. To write a reply, Pip sends the model the recent conversation, the business's facts and instructions, and the results of any tool the teammate used. Anthropic's commercial terms do not allow it to train its models on this content.

AI can be wrong. A teammate is instructed never to say something was booked, sent or saved unless it was. Bookings and drafts that need the owner's approval are sent to the owner and are not final until the owner confirms.

No one is denied service, credit, housing, a job or insurance by a Pip teammate's decision. Teammates answer questions, take details and do routine tasks.

## 7. How we use information

- To run the teammates a business ordered and show the business what they did.
- To bill the business and keep its account working.
- To honor opt-outs and keep proof of consent.
- To keep the service safe, stop abuse and fix problems.
- To meet legal duties.
- To build better teammates. When a teammate has been tested, we may keep its design as a pattern for future teammates, with business names, sites, people, phone numbers, emails and addresses replaced by placeholders, and with the business's facts removed.

We do not sell personal information. We do not share it for advertising. We do not build profiles of a business's customers.

## 10. Your choices

- **Stop texts and calls:** reply STOP, or tell the teammate on any channel. It applies to every channel for that business.
- **See, correct or delete your information:** email [privacy@hirepip.com] or ask the business. We answer within 45 days. We may need to confirm it is you, usually by replying to the number or address you used.
- **Business owners:** you can disconnect Pip and delete everything from the Portal at any time.
- **State privacy rights:** depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete and get a copy of your information, and to appeal if we say no. We honor these requests for everyone, wherever they live. We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising, so there is nothing to opt out of.

## 11. Children

Pip is for businesses and their adult customers. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given a teammate personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

## 12. Security

Sign-ins and keys for a business's tools are held by a provider built for that purpose and are never shown to an AI model. Access to Pip's database is limited to the service itself and the founder. Data is encrypted in transit. No system is perfectly secure; if there is a breach that affects you, we will tell you as the law requires.

## 13. Where information is processed

In the United States. Pip is offered only to businesses in the United States.
