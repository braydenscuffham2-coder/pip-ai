# Pip Data Processing Addendum

> **DRAFT. Not legal advice. Not reviewed by a lawyer. Not published.** Lane 10, November 17, 2026.

This addendum is part of the Pip Terms of Service. It covers personal information about your customers that Pip handles for you ("Customer Data").

## 1. Roles

You decide why and how Customer Data is used. Pip is your service provider (also called a processor) and handles Customer Data only on your instructions. Your instructions are: the Terms, your Order, your teammates' settings, and what you do in the Portal.

For your own account, sign-in and billing details, Pip decides how they are used, as the Privacy Policy says.

## 2. What is handled

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Whose data** | People who chat with, text, call or email your teammates; contacts you upload |
| **What** | Message and call content; name, phone, email and requests they give; consent and opt-out records; whatever a teammate adds to or reads from the tools you connect, within its limits |
| **Why** | To answer your customers and do the jobs in your Order |
| **How long** | For the life of your account, then deleted as section 8 says |
| **Sensitive data** | Not requested. Teammates are told not to ask for card numbers, passwords, government IDs or health details. Pip is not for protected health information without a separate signed agreement. |

## 3. What Pip will do

- Use Customer Data only to provide the service to you. Not sell it, not share it for advertising, not combine it with data from other clients, and not use it to train AI models.
- Keep it confidential and make sure anyone with access is bound to do the same.
- Keep reasonable security in place (section 5).
- Tell you if we believe an instruction breaks the law.
- Help you answer a person's request to see, correct or delete their data. If the person contacts us, we pass the request to you.
- Tell you without undue delay, and within [72 hours] of confirming it, if Customer Data is accessed or disclosed without authorization, with what we know. [LAWYER: confirm period.]

## 5. Security

- Tool sign-ins and keys are held by a dedicated provider and never shown to an AI model or stored in Pip's database.
- Phone numbers in consent records are stored as one-way codes plus the last four digits.
- Database access is limited to the service and the founder; row-level security is on for every table.
- Data is encrypted in transit.
- Sensitive Portal actions require a fresh sign-in.

## 6. Your duties

You confirm you have the right to give Pip the Customer Data and contact lists you provide, that your own privacy notice covers using a service provider, and that you have any consent the law requires to contact your customers.

## 7. Checks

On request, once a year, Pip will answer a reasonable written security questionnaire. [LAWYER: enough for small clients, or is an audit right needed for clients covered by state privacy laws?]

## 8. Return and deletion

You can delete everything from the Portal at any time. Pip then deletes Customer Data, except consent and opt-out records (kept as one-way codes), the compliance log and signed agreements, which are kept for up to four years so opt-outs keep working and both of us can show what was agreed.
