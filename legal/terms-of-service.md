## 2. What Pip does

A teammate is software that uses an AI model to answer your customers and do routine tasks in the tools you connect. Depending on your Order it may answer website chat, texts and calls, take leads and booking requests, check your calendar, add records to your tools, and send email from your address.

**Teammates are autopilot.** After setup they act without asking you first, following the instructions and limits agreed at setup. Before a teammate goes live, the Portal shows you in plain words what it can and cannot do. Going live means you have read that list and approve it.

**What our software enforces today.** [LAWYER AND LANE 01: this list must match the code on the day of publishing.]

- Every first message says the teammate is an AI assistant.
- A teammate can only use the tools it was built with, and only the connections that are working.
- A teammate may only send email to an address your customer typed in that conversation.
- Calendar checks show the teammate busy and free times only.
- In a tool you connect beyond the setup steps, a teammate may only add one new record at a time. It cannot read, change or delete what is there.
- A teammate never opens payment, banking, payroll or accounting tools.
- Booking requests and drafts go to you for approval and are not final until you confirm.
- If a connection stops working, that one job pauses and you are alerted. Other jobs keep running.

Anything not on this list is an instruction to the AI, not a guarantee.

**AI can be wrong.** A teammate may misunderstand a customer or state something about your business incorrectly. You agree to give accurate business facts, keep them current, and check the activity log in your Portal.

- Telling your customers, in your own privacy notice, that you use a service provider to answer messages.
- Keeping your Portal sign-in to yourself and your staff.

## 4. Your tools and sign-ins

You connect tools by signing in on a page run by our connection provider. Pip and the AI models never see your passwords or keys. You authorize Pip to act in each connected tool on your behalf, within the limits in section 2 and your Order. You can disconnect any tool at any time.

## 5. Calls, texts and email

- Every teammate says it is an AI at the start of each call, text conversation and chat. You may not ask us to turn this off.
- If calls are recorded, the teammate says so at the start of the call.
- Teammates reply to people who contact you. They place outbound calls and send marketing texts only to contacts with a stored consent.
- If you upload contacts, you confirm each one asked to hear from you. Uploaded contacts can receive service messages only.
- An opt-out on any channel stops calls and texts from your Pip number on every channel, at once. You may not ask us to contact someone who opted out.
- You give us accurate details for carrier registration and agree we may submit them for you.
- Teammates do not handle emergencies. They tell the caller to hang up and dial 911.

- **No cut-off for usage.** A busy month never pauses your teammates.
- **Late payment.** If an invoice is unpaid 7 days after it is due, we may pause your teammates until it is paid.
- **Changes.** We will email you at least 30 days before any price change. [LAWYER: confirm notice period.]
