# Brain engine 4: design for what the business deserves (Nov 23, 2026)

Shipped in PR #78. Live on www.hirepip.com and in the Marketplace artifact (version `1795422141-a5af`).

## What changed

1. **The engine prompt** (`scripts/brain/engine-prompt.txt`). It used to say "using only building blocks that exist in the real world today". It now asks for the team the business deserves, and lets an idea name a part Pip has not built yet in a new field, `new_capability`. New lenses: the whole role, foresight, memory, the frontier. New portfolio rules: at least three frontier ideas, one whole-role idea, a front-door teammate when nobody can answer, and at most one competitor-watching and one review pick.
2. **Ranking.** The old score multiplied a penalty for every part that still had to be built (ready 1, needs setup 0.9, build 0.7, hardware 0.5) and divided by effort. A phone desk scored about 0.22 of a website chat with the same evidence, which is why two businesses that plainly needed one did not get it. Now the hardest part sets a mild discount (1, 0.97, 0.9, 0.75), effort is a mild divisor, and the engine's own order counts (first idea 1.4x, ninth and later 1x).
3. **House picks.** Competitor watching and review asking or replying: one of each at most, with a discount (0.8 if the engine ranked it in its first six, 0.45 if not).
4. **Honesty.** A pick that needs a new part says so in its setup ("Pip builds this before go-live") and in one FAQ answer, and its badge reads "Built for you" instead of "Ready in 2-3 weeks". No dates, no prices.
5. **Robustness.** If the engine writes its answer twice, the last complete answer is used.
6. **One source.** `python3 scripts/brain/patch_brain.py <file>` applies the same prompt and rules to `public/app.html`, the saved artifact and `claude-app/modules/brain.js`. `tests/site/brain-inpage.test.mjs` loads the Brain out of `public/app.html` and fails if a copy drifts.

## Known and not fixed here

- **The engine call is close to its 300 s limit at slow hours.** On Nov 23 around 07:30 UTC the old prompt took 227 s and the new one 234 s for the same business; earlier that night both took about 95 s. The slowdown is the model service, not the prompt, but a call that passes 300 s falls back to the weaker single-prompt design.
- **A stray request to `/[object Object]`** (404) fires when the Market step opens. It is on the old page too.
- **The team total moved between two identical runs** ($3,400 to $6,800 at 1280px, $3,200 to $6,400 at 360px).
- **"Ready in 2-3 weeks"** still shows on picks that need no new part. That is a date Pip is promising; texting alone needs carrier registration that takes 2 to 4 weeks.
- **Review history depends on the DataForSEO day line** ($3). In the baseline, six of ten scans read only 5 reviews.
- **The spend meter was losing calls** (fixed in PR #79). On Nov 23, four engine-sized test calls, about $3, were never counted, so that day's real page spend is about $3 above what the meter shows.
- **Preview deployments cannot run the Brain** (no model key there), so Brain changes are tested with the preview page calling the production model route.
- The artifact copy was republished with the identical Brain block but could not be opened in the Claude app from the build session.
